The problem behind most calls we get

The work is finished. Why are you still chasing the money?

You shouldn't have to become a collections expert just because a customer refuses to pay. When invoices go unpaid, you may be left wondering:

  • How many reminders should I send?
  • When should I put a formal demand in writing?
  • Could a mechanic's lien apply to this job?
  • What happens if the customer still won't pay?
  • How can I handle overdue accounts more consistently in the future?

The longer an invoice remains unresolved, the more frustrating the situation can become. And when legal deadlines apply, timing matters.

You need a clear process, not another round of guesswork.
