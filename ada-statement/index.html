TradeWise Legal, PLLC is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all individuals, including people with disabilities. We strive to provide a website that is accessible, user-friendly, and inclusive for all visitors.

Our Commitment

We continually work to improve the accessibility and usability of our website and follow generally recognized accessibility standards and best practices whenever reasonably possible.

Accessibility Features

We strive to maintain website features that support accessibility, including:

*Clear and consistent navigation

*Readable text and content structure

*Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

*Alternative text for meaningful images where appropriate

*Keyboard-accessible functionality where possible

*Ongoing review and improvement of website accessibility

Ongoing Improvements

Website accessibility is an ongoing effort. We regularly review our website and make updates designed to improve accessibility and usability for all users.

While we strive to ensure that all content is accessible, some areas of the website may not yet fully meet every accessibility standard. We welcome feedback that helps us identify and address accessibility barriers.

Need Assistance?

If you experience difficulty accessing any content on this website, encounter an accessibility barrier, or need assistance obtaining information available on this website, please contact us using the information below.

We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information, service, or resource you need through an alternative communication method or to improve accessibility where appropriate.
