Most plumbing businesses need legal documents in three key areas

Built to help you run your business better

A plumbing company usually has three different relationships to manage from a legal standpoint.

There is the business itself, including ownership documents, company records, licenses, and compliance.

There is your team, whether that means employees, independent contractors, office staff, technicians, or a combination of all of them.

Then there are your customers, where good paperwork can help make the scope of the job, pricing, payment terms, changes, and warranties much clearer from the beginning.

The Business Essentials Package brings those three areas together so your legal paperwork is not scattered across old files, generic forms, and documents that were created years apart.

Employee & independent contractor documents

Growing your crew shouldn't create a new set of problems

There usually comes a point when you can't do everything yourself.

Maybe you're hiring technicians. Maybe you're bringing in office staff. Maybe you're considering using independent contractors. The distinction matters.

How someone works with your company can affect taxes, documentation, workplace policies and your responsibilities as the business owner. Simply calling someone a “1099 contractor” doesn't necessarily make that person an independent contractor.

The Business Essentials Package gives you documents and guidance to create a more consistent process when adding people to your company.

Instead of handling every new worker differently, you'll have a repeatable framework for documenting the relationship from the beginning.

Customer agreements & job paperwork

The best time to avoid a customer dispute is before the work begins

Some of the most frustrating disputes in a plumbing business start with conversations everyone thought were perfectly clear.

  • What exactly was included?
  • Was that extra work part of the original quote?
  • When was payment due?
  • Did the customer approve the additional cost?
  • What does the warranty actually cover?

When those details live only in a phone call or someone's memory, a small misunderstanding can become a much larger argument.

Business Essentials gives your company reusable customer-facing documents designed to put those details in writing.
