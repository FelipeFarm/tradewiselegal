How to get your legal documents with TradeWise Legal

What happens after you submit your request

  1. 1

    We review your request

    Once we receive your request, TradeWise Legal reviews your details to confirm that the correct package or document fits the situation and to flag anything else that might be needed based on what you’ve told us. If your package includes a consultation call, that’s scheduled first, so anything specific to your business can be addressed before documents are drafted.

  2. 2

    We prepare your documents

    Next, your documents are prepared. Turnaround time depends on the complexity of the request. À la carte documents and smaller packages generally have a faster turnaround than packages involving multiple documents or a filing process, such as trademark applications or succession plans, which take longer due to the searches, reviews, or drafting involved.

  3. 3

    We deliver your documents and handle follow-up

    Once ready, your documents are delivered by email along with clear instructions for how to use them. Any deadline-sensitive items are flagged so nothing gets missed. If something requires clarification or correction after delivery, follow-up is part of our process. We’ll make sure you understand your documents and address any necessary adjustments related to your original request.

What we need from you to prepare your legal documents

A few details. That's it.

Getting started is quick and easy, with no lengthy intake required. In order to prepare your documents accurately, TradeWise needs a short description of your issue or situation, basic business information (business name, entity type, and state of formation), and any relevant details specific to the request, such as the unpaid amount and customer information for a collections matter, or the property address and job details for a lien filing.

WHEN EXTRA INFO HELPS

For some packages, providing additional information will speed things up. A trademark filing can benefit from knowing the exact name, logo, or tagline you want protected, and the date you first used it in business. With a succession or estate planning package, it’s helpful to know your ownership structure and any family or partner considerations up front, since that determines which documents apply to your situation.
