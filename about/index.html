Our story

Legal support built for the way trades businesses actually work

TradeWise Legal was founded with a simple idea: getting reliable legal help shouldn't be complicated, time-consuming, or pull business owners away from the work that keeps their companies running.

Founding attorney Carey Worrell has spent years supporting business owners across Texas. Through that work, she saw a clear gap in traditional legal services, particularly for owners in the home services industry. Too often, getting an answer to a straightforward legal question meant dealing with hourly billing, navigating unnecessary complexity, or working with documents and advice that weren't designed for the realities of running a trades business.

As a business owner herself, Carey knows that legal issues are only one part of an owner's day. There are employees to manage, customers to serve, jobs to complete, invoices to collect, and a business to grow. TradeWise Legal was created to make the legal side simpler, with practical guidance, straightforward pricing, and services designed to help owners protect what they've built without getting buried in legal guesswork.
